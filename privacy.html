Recursive Find — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Recursive Find is a browser extension that searches for text on the page you are viewing and, to a depth you choose, on the pages that page links to. This policy explains what the extension accesses and what it does (and does not) do with that information.

The short version: Recursive Find does all of its work locally in your browser. It does not collect, store off your device, sell, or transmit any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no external servers operated by us.

What the extension accesses

  • The current page's text and links. When you start a search, the extension reads the visible text and hyperlinks of the tab you are viewing so it knows what to search and which links to follow.
  • The content of linked pages. To perform a recursive search, the extension fetches the linked pages (which may be on any website) and searches their text. This is the same kind of request your browser makes when you click a link.
  • Your search settings. Your last-used query, depth, page limit, match mode, and toggle options are saved on your device (via chrome.storage.local) so they persist between sessions.

What we do with it

All page content is processed in memory in your browser purely to produce and display search results, and is discarded when the search ends. None of it is sent to us or to any third party. We do not build profiles, run analytics, or log your activity.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — read the current page's text and links, and highlight a match in a result page you open.
  • host access (all sites) — fetch the text of pages linked from your current page during a search you start. The set of sites isn't known in advance because it depends on the page you're viewing.
  • storage — remember your search settings locally.
  • sidePanel — display the extension's side-panel interface.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties. We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
